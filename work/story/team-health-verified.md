# Team Health Verified: Locked Library Door Beats

## Beat 1: The Threshold of Knowledge

The traveler stands before the great oak doors of the library, their fingers tracing the carved initials of generations past. The iron keyhole is rusted shut, a silent testament to the building's long abandonment.

> "They say the last librarian vanished into the stacks," the traveler murmurs, voice barely audible against the wind. "Or perhaps they never left at all."

A faint glow emanates from beneath the door—warm, amber light that pulses like a heartbeat. The traveler hesitates, one hand hovering over the cold metal of the doorframe.

**Player Choice:**
- **Knock gently** — Respect the silence, listen for a response
- **Force the door open** — Knowledge waits for no one
- **Search for another entrance** — There must be a way in without breaking in

## Beat 2: The Whispering Stacks

The traveler steps inside, the door clicking shut behind them. Dust motes dance in shafts of light that pierce the gloom. Books line walls from floor to ceiling, their spines cracked and faded.

> "Hello?" the traveler calls out, but the only answer is the settling of the building.

Then—a sound. Not from the books, but from the walls themselves. A low hum, like the library is breathing. The traveler's shadow stretches unnaturally long across the floor, seeming to detach from their body.

**Player Choice:**
- **Follow the light** — The glow seems to be moving deeper into the stacks
- **Investigate the hum** — Something is wrong with the building's structure
- **Check the shadow** — Is that really their own reflection?
