# TEST/NON-CANON

## Opening

Rain lashed against the iron archive doors as Mara and Ivo stood beneath the eaves, the sealed letter clutched between them. The brass handle gleamed wet in the streetlamp's glow. Mara, 32 and her fingers calloused from years with keys, glanced at Ivo—35, archivist by trade, patient by nature.

"The seal must not break," Ivo murmured, shielding the parchment from the deluge with his palm. The heavy door stood LOCKED, a barrier between them and the dry interior.

## Choice A

Mara withdrew a slim lockpick from her pocket and slid it into the keyhole. The mechanism clicked free with a satisfying metallic click. She rapped the bell overhead; the caretaker emerged moments later, eyes squinting at the two strangers.

"The archive? I thought you'd wait for tomorrow," he asked, water dripping from his cap.

"Yes," Mara said. "But the letter must enter dry."

The caretaker nodded and ushered them both inside, the heavy doors sealing behind them. He guided them down to the reading room, where Ivo's letter emerged perfectly DRY from the storm.

## Choice B

Ivo knocked the door instead, his knuckles waiting patiently against the cold wood. The bell chimed softly; Ivo waited still as the caretaker appeared, this time with a dry handkerchief in one pocket.

"The library is open," he said, admitting both of them inside. He guided them to the reading room, where Ivo's letter emerged DAMP but its seal perfectly intact from the storm.

## Shared Rejoin

They settled into their respective chairs in the reading room, the letter between them. Whether it had survived the storm intact or been gently protected by the caretaker's care, the moment remained suspended in the quiet of the archive.
