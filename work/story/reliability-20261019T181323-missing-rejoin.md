# TEST / NON-CANON

## Opening
Mara (32, locksmith) and Ivo (35, archivist) wait outside a locked archive in rain, carrying a sealed letter. Choose exactly one alternative.

## Choice A
Mara picks the lock. A bell summons the caretaker, who opens the door, admits both characters, and guides them to the reading room. Mara keeps the letter dry. They sit beside the reading table.

## Choice B
Ivo knocks and waits. The caretaker opens the locked door, admits both characters, and guides them to the reading room. Rain dampens the letter exterior but its seal stays intact. They sit beside the reading table.
