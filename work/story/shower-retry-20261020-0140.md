# Prototype: The Locked Library Door

The rain hammered against cobblestones as Elara pulled her cloak tighter. The town square had emptied hours ago, leaving only distant streetlamp glows and rhythmic drumming water. She'd sought shelter when she noticed the peculiar structure: a small, ivy-covered building with a heavy oak door bearing no handle, only a brass keyhole.

She stood firmly outside, hands in her pockets. The door remained locked. Inside, through the wood grain, she could sense old paper and dried lavender. Bookshelves likely lined walls, reaching toward a vaulted ceiling. A single lantern hung inside, its flame flickering with unnatural blue.

**Choice 1: Pick the lock**

Elara's training as a thief surfaced. She pulled a thin wire from her belt, fingers moving with practiced precision. The lock clicked open with a satisfying snap. She pushed the door inward and stepped inside the main hall.

The room beyond held books arranged in perfect geometric patterns, spines facing outward. In the center stood a pedestal with a single glowing orb. As she approached, the air felt still. The orb pulsed warmly, and Elara felt knowledge flooding her mind—ancient languages, forgotten histories, secrets of the stars. But the books remained empty shells, their pages blank. She'd gained power, but lost the opportunity to learn from the texts themselves.

**Choice 2: Seek help in town**

Elara hesitated, then decided against forcing entry. She turned her attention to the empty streets. Perhaps the storm had driven someone else to seek shelter, or maybe someone knew about this building's strange energy.

She wandered through town, checking each house for signs of life. In the baker's shop, she found bread still warm from the oven. At the blacksmith's, she discovered a map pinned to the wall showing underground tunnels beneath the town.

**Consequence:** The map revealed a hidden entrance accessible through the town's old sewer system. Elara realized the library wasn't meant to be entered from the street—it was designed to deter casual visitors. The locked door was intentional, forcing those who truly sought knowledge to find the proper entrance.

**Rejoining Point:**

Elara stood before the library's basement entrance, visible through the sewer grate beneath the building. Both paths—the surface deception and underground reality—led to this same location. She descended into the darkness, knowing whatever she found below would change her understanding of the library's purpose. The rain continued above, but beneath the earth, the real story awaited.
