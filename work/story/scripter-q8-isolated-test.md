## Mara & Ivo: The Locked Archive

**Characters:** Mara (32, locksmith) and Ivo (35, archivist)  
**Setting:** Outside the locked archive, rain falling  
**Status:** TEST / NON-CANON

---

The rain slicks the stone steps as Mara and Ivo stand before the heavy oak doors of the archive. A sealed letter rests in Mara's hands, the wax unblemished despite the downpour.

Ivo shifts impatiently. "Should we just—"

### Option A: The Lock Picked
Mara pulls a slender tool from her pocket, the metal gleaming against the gray light. With a practiced twist, the lock clicks open and a brass bell chimes overhead, summoning the caretaker.

The caretaker appears at the door, surprised by the noise. "Ah, you were waiting!" He ushers both inside, the bell's echo fading behind them.

In the reading room, Mara's letter remains pristine in her grip. She and Ivo settle into worn leather chairs, the rain now a distant drumming against the glass.

### Option B: The Gentle Knock
Ivo reaches for the door, raising his knuckles. He waits as the caretaker descends, opening with a soft, deliberate turn. The bell never rings.

As they step inside, Mara's letter catches the drizzle—water blooms across the wax seal, darkening the crest. The caretaker offers no apology.

They find the same reading room, Ivo now seated beside Mara. He gestures at her letter. "Is it still readable?" She hesitates, then smiles.
