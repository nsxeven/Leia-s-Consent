## Original sounds

Synthesised by the Leia's Consent team (numpy, `build_assets.py`); fully owned by the project:

`sfx/cloth_change` (`tools/gen_sfx_cloth.py`), `sfx/heartbeat_fast`, `sfx/heartbeat_slow`, `sfx/timer_beeps`, `ui/app_tap`, `ui/back`, `ui/bar`, `ui/gain`, `ui/loss`, `ui/message_pop`, `ui/paper`, `ui/phone_close`, `ui/phone_open`, `ui/pick`, `ui/polaroid`, `ui/rustle`, `ui/save`, `ui/tap`, `ui/thud`, `ui/tick`

Processing: trimmed, faded, loudness-balanced and encoded to OGG Vorbis 44.1 kHz. Ambience beds and music are seamless loops. See `manifest.json` for per-file notes.

Name-card typing sounds `ui/key_1`..`ui/key_6`, `ui/key_back`, `ui/key_enter`: cut from [Keyboard single key presses.wav](https://freesound.org/people/5ro4/sounds/609534/) (#609534) by 5ro4, CC0 1.0.

Polaroid hover sound `ui/tock`: synthesised in-house (original work, no third-party source).

Drink splash `sfx/drink_spill`: supplied by the project developer (replaces the earlier Freesound spill).

Crowd chatter `amb/crowd` (Prologue party): supplied by the project developer.

Squish `sfx/squish` (supplied by the project developer): kept in the library, no longer played; the nightmare dinner CG now uses `lewd/cum_in01` (below).

**Lewd sounds by Shinlalala.** 61 sound effects from *Shinlalala's Lewd Sound Pack* © 2024 by Shinlalala, licensed under
[CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Files are unmodified and keep their original names:
`lewd/cum_in01`..`cum_in03`, `lewd/cum_out01`..`cum_out06`, `lewd/plapdry01`..`plapdry11`, `lewd/plapwet01`..`plapwet16`,
`lewd/pullout01`..`pullout03`, `lewd/stroke01`..`stroke10`, `lewd/wet01`..`wet12`. Any edited version of these files must
also be shared under CC BY-SA 4.0 and keep this credit.
