# Maya — DRAFT

## Snapshot
Ryan's longtime friend from back home *(canon)*; athletic, socially confident,
fashion-forward *(canon)*. Happens to be on the same island; first meets Leia in
person on Day 2 *(canon)*. Becomes Leia's friend almost instantly instead of a
rival *(canon)*. Notices things other people miss, including Ryan withdrawing,
and tries to protect the relationship *(canon)*. Into Julian, and says so
*(in game, Day 2 19b)*.

She's a real friend, not a plot device: she can push, but Leia always chooses
*(canon)*.

## Wants / fears
- Wants: fun, honesty, people to stop being so careful; Julian (more than she
  admits).
- Fears: boredom; people not saying what they mean.

## How she talks
- **Fast and chatty.** Interrupts, finishes people's sentences, changes topics
  mid-thought and comes back to them.
- Asks blunt questions nobody else would ("So are you two experimenting or what?").
- Hype-woman: big reactions, "oh my God", "stop it", "no because...".
- Teases Julian constantly; her roasts are longer and more creative than
  everyone else's, not one-word.
- Gets serious suddenly and drops the jokes when someone she cares about is hurting.
