# Julian — DRAFT

## Snapshot
27. Ryan's longtime friend; wealthy, socially confident, heavyset/chubby
*(canon)*. Paid for the villa and won't let anyone forget it, as a joke *(in game)*.
Wingman who got Ryan and Leia together *(in game)*. Terrified of bees *(canon)*.
Sunglasses are "his personality" according to Maya *(in game)*.

Respectful and loyal to Ryan early on; any change in how he behaves around Leia
is slow and earned *(canon: "should not become suddenly predatory")*.

## Wants / fears
- Wants: everybody to have a good time on *his* trip; to be the host; to be liked.
- Fears: being the third wheel; being seen as just "the rich friend"; bees.
- Under the bluster: a bit lonely, which Maya picks up on.

## How he talks
- **The loud one.** Longest sentences in the cast. Rambles, exaggerates,
  narrates his own plans, over-explains a joke.
- Big-brother energy with Ryan. Bro-ish but not dumb.
- Comic panic: when scared or caught out he talks *more*, not less. ("No. No no
  no. Is it on me? Ryan. RYAN. Is it on me?")
- Defensive honesty when it matters: "Look, I'm just saying, I didn't see
  anything, and if I did see anything, which I didn't..."
- He is the opposite of "man of few words". Give his scenes a different rhythm.

## Under pressure
| Situation | How he sounds |
|---|---|
| Nervous | Babbles, jokes too much, overcorrects. |
| Flirting (Maya) | Tries smooth, fails, recovers with self-mockery. |
| Caught looking at Leia | Over-apologizes, rambles, looks anywhere else. |
| Drunk | Philosophical, then sentimental, then asleep. |
| Aroused (later routes) | Surprised at himself first; checks with Ryan and Leia. |
| Angry | Rare; goes quiet and serious, which scares everyone. |

## With each person
- **Ryan**: oldest friend; roasting plus genuine loyalty. Tells Ryan hard truths when sober.
- **Leia**: friendly, gentlemanly, careful; becomes aware of her over the trip.
- **Maya**: sparring partner; she out-talks him and he likes it.

## By route
- **Love**: comic relief and good friend.
- **Love + Exhibitionism**: flustered bystander who very much doesn't know where to look.
- **NTS**: "roped in" later. Asks permission twice, can't believe his luck,
  ends up part of it. Never pushes.
- **NTR**: does not chase Leia; the affair partner is someone else (Adrian Vale
  or another island character). Julian's loyalty can be a painful contrast.

## Says / never says
- Says: "Okay, hear me out.", "I'm not saying I'm right, I'm saying I'm not wrong.", "Bee. BEE."
- Never: cool short verdict lines; predatory or smug lines about Leia; "There she is."

## Sentence length
- Typical spoken line: **10-30 words**, average about 14-18. The longest lines in the cast.
- Short lines: **about 15-25%**, mostly comic panic ("Bee. BEE.") or the rare serious moment.
- **Serious mode is the exception**: short, quiet, no jokes. It scares people because it's rare.

## Real-speech habits (from the evidence)
- **Long turns built from short pieces**: setup, detour, "anyway", and a big finish [C02-C05].
- **Hosts out loud**: "we", "everyone", group plans, checking that people are having fun [P05].
- **Roasts lean on shared history** ("remember the thing with the karaoke machine", invented example), not clever
  wording, and he loses as often as he wins [R15, R11].
- **Comic panic means more words, not fewer**, plus repetition [C08 repair, R12].
- **Laughs at his own jokes first**, often before the punchline lands [C12].
- **Keeps old arguments alive as running jokes** with Ryan [R11].
