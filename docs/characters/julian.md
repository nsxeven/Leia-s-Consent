## Wants / fears
- Wants: everybody to have a good time; to be liked for himself, not his money.

## How he talks
- **Long, easygoing turns, not loud ones.** The longest lines in the cast, but they come
  from explaining, telling a story or over-explaining when embarrassed, never from
  showing off. Warm, mid-volume, self-deprecating.
- **Plays the money down:** "It's honestly not a big deal", "my dad's thing, not mine",
  then changes the subject to someone else.
- **Shy mode** (teased about money or his size): his lines go short and quiet, he goes red,
  looks away, says "Can we not?", or hands the topic to someone else. It's the one time
  he's at a loss for words.
- Big-brother energy with Ryan, bro-ish but not dumb.
- **Bee panic is the exception** where he does get loud ("No. No no no. Is it on me?").
  It's funny *because* he's usually so mellow.
- Defensive honesty when it matters: "Look, I didn't see anything, and if I did see
  anything, which I didn't..."
