# Leia — DRAFT

## Snapshot
25. Korean, pale skin, long pastel-pink hair, blue eyes *(canon)*. Four years with
Ryan. Affectionate, playful, a little self-conscious about attention, and
genuinely not looking to cheat at the start *(canon)*. Hates folding laundry and
will "live out of a clean laundry basket for a week" *(in game)*. Over-packs,
changes outfits, gets "meaner on holiday" (teasing, not cruel) *(in game)*.

The story is her **discovering** what she likes. She's not a secret slut waiting
to be revealed and she's not a doll things happen to: she chooses.

## Wants / fears
- Wants: to feel wanted and seen, especially by Ryan; to have fun; to be brave
  for once.
- Fears: being judged; looking silly; Ryan going cold on her.
- Tell: she checks Ryan's face after anything bold. Whether she still does that
  is the clearest sign of which route you're on.

## How she talks
- The most talkative of the couple. Runs sentences together when excited, uses
  "like", "literally", "okay but", "wait wait wait".
- Laughs at her own jokes, sometimes mid-sentence. Narration can show it
  instead of her saying "haha".
- Teases Ryan constantly but warmly; her insults are affection ("you're such a
  dork", "pervert" said while smiling).
- When embarrassed, she gets louder and changes the subject, or goes very quiet
  and very short. Those two modes, not a constant cool deadpan.
- Asks questions back. Wonders out loud. Admits things in pieces.

## With each person
- **Ryan**: home base. Teases, leans on him, checks his face.
- **Maya**: new best friend fast; girl-talk mode, more daring when Maya hypes her up.
- **Julian**: friendly, a bit of sisterly bullying, and later, depending on the
  route, awareness of how he looks at her.
- **Strangers**: polite and shy at first.

## By route
- **Love**: says "us" a lot; sweet, private, still playful.
- **Love + Exhibitionism**: "I can't believe we did that", a giggly co-conspirator with Ryan.
- **NTS**: grows confident. Submissive: lets herself be led, wants Ryan there.
  Tease Queen: runs the room, loves making Ryan watch. Always checks in with Ryan
  at each new step.
- **NTR**: reaches for Ryan, gets nothing, stops reaching. Secretive later, and
  her guilt shows in her voice before it shows in what she does.

## Says / never says
- Says: "Okay, but listen.", "Don't laugh.", "Wait, are you serious?", "I'm being so weird right now."
- Never: deadpan one-word verdicts in a row ("Exactly." "Pervert." "Fine."),
  cool-girl lines with no feeling behind them, lines that explain her own subtext.

## Sentence length
- Typical spoken line: **6-20 words**, average about 10-13. The longest lines in the couple.
- Short lines (≤4 words): **about 20-30%**, mostly reactions ("Wait, what?", "Oh my God.").
- Goes **longer and faster** when excited or nervous; goes **very short** only when hurt
  ("it's fine") or in the middle of intimacy.

## Real-speech habits (from the evidence)
- **Runs thoughts together** with "and", "like" and "okay but", then adds one more thought
  after she seems done [C02-C05 long turns are chains of 3-4-word pieces].
- **Laughs at her own lines**, often mid-sentence, before the listener does [C12].
- **Corrects herself out loud**: "It's not— okay, it's a little bit see-through." [C08].
- **Hard things come out as a hedged question first**: "Is it weird if...?", "Can I say
  something without you being weird about it?" [R08, R04].
- **Jealousy comes out sideways**, as a loaded question that sounds casual ("So is she, like,
  funny, or...?") before she admits the real feeling [R06].
- **New-friend mode with Maya**: compliment first, then something they have in common, then
  over-sharing, then "sorry, is that too much?" [R16].
- **When she suggests something new**, she says her own fear first ("you'll think I'm being
  weird") and leaves a clear way out ("we don't have to") [R04, R01].
- **Running bit with Ryan (darryn's pick): "two minutes".** She's always "two minutes" from
  ready; he counts them out loud ("That's four minutes. Five.") and she pretends not to hear.
  It comes back all trip, and the payoff is her groan, not a comeback [R10].
