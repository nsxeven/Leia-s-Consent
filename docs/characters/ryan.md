# Ryan (player character) — DRAFT

## Snapshot
26. The narrator: we hear his head all the time, so his *spoken* lines can be
plainer than his thoughts. Four years with Leia; got together at a college party
thanks to Julian and a Truth or Dare *(in game)*. Practiced asking her out in a
mirror for three weeks *(Day 5 draft)*. A normal, decent guy who loves her, is a
bit out of his depth on this trip, and sometimes handles it badly.

He is **not** cool. He's not a quip machine and he doesn't win every exchange.
Competent where it counts (looks after people, makes decisions, can take
charge), awkward in the small moments.

## Wants / fears
- Wants: Leia happy, the trip to go well, to feel like he's enough for her.
- Fears: being boring; being the jealous guy; losing her without noticing.
- Blind spot: goes quiet and "fine" instead of saying what's wrong. That is the
  root of the NTR route, so it has to feel human, not cruel.

## How he talks
- Medium sentences, plain words. Starts lines with "I mean", "Okay, so",
  "Wait", "No, yeah". Trails off when unsure ("It's just... I don't know").
- Asks real questions instead of firing back one-liners.
- Humor: dry, self-deprecating, mostly at his own expense. Once in a while, not
  every exchange. Often the joke falls flat and Leia says so.
- Says what he feels clumsily rather than precisely. Over-explains when guilty.
- In the narration (his inner voice) he can be funnier and more observant than
  he is out loud. That contrast is part of him.

## Under pressure
| Situation | How he sounds |
|---|---|
| Nervous | Rambles, repeats himself, checks: "Is that okay? I mean, is that... okay?" |
| Flirting with Leia | Warm, a little corny, sincere. Compliments that are too honest. |
| Jealous | Goes quiet, short answers, "it's fine" (NTR seed). Or blurts and regrets it. |
| Drunk | Louder, sentimental, tells Julian he loves him. |
| Aroused / intimate | Direct and simple, checks in with her, takes charge when she asks him to. |
| Hurt | Says less, avoids eye contact, cracks a weak joke to get out of it. |
| Claim-back (NTS) | Calm and in control: short and sure, never mean. "Come here." It's earned and agreed. |

## With each person
- **Leia**: soft, teasing in an affectionate way, private jokes, nicknames ("babe").
- **Julian**: old-friend shorthand, roasts each other, but Ryan's honest with him when it matters.
- **Maya**: easy, sibling-ish, a little defensive when she reads him too well.

## By route
- **Love**: present, attentive, the one she runs back to.
- **Love + Exhibitionism**: nervous-excited, in on it, the two of them sharing a secret.
- **NTS**: grows from "are we really doing this?" to confident. Submissive variant:
  joins in. Tease Queen variant: watches. Claim-back at night.
- **NTR**: honest mistakes, not cruelty: oblivious, distracted, jealous,
  controlling, "I'm fine." Later: discovers, watches through the spy app,
  chooses to hold on or let go.

## Says / never says
- Says: "Wait, what?", "Sorry, that came out wrong.", "You okay?", "I don't know, I just..."
- Never: "There she is." "Exactly." "Noted." Smug one-word verdicts. Lines that
  sound like a movie trailer. Mean or contemptuous lines on purpose (unless the
  scene is about him regretting it).

- **Big Five:** Openness mid, Conscientiousness mid, Extraversion low-mid, Agreeableness high,

## Sentence length
- Typical spoken line: **4-12 words**, average about 6-8. Shorter than Leia and Julian,
  **but never a deadpan one-word verdict** ("Exactly." "Noted.").
- Short lines: **about 30-40%**, mostly plain reactions and questions ("Wait, what?", "You okay?").
- Goes **long only when nervous or guilty**: then he over-explains in chained clauses (15-25 words).
- His narration can stay sharper and funnier than his speech; that gap is part of him.

## Real-speech habits (from the evidence)
- **"I mean" before he corrects himself**: "I'm not— I mean, I was looking. Sorry." [C11, C08].
- **Answers teases literally or with a groan** more than with a comeback [C13, R10]. Gets maybe
  one good line per scene, if that [R12].
- **Serious talk arrives sideways with Julian**, in the middle of doing something, and leaves
  fast [R15].
- **Short reassurance, not speeches**: "Yeah. We're good. Come here." [R08, R17].
- **"I'm fine, just tired."** plus turning away is the NTR seed. He doesn't name the feeling [P11, R17].
- **Running bit with Leia (darryn's pick): "two minutes".** When she says she'll be "two
  minutes", he counts out loud ("That's four. Five. We're at five, babe.") and she ignores him.
  Use it all trip, sometimes sweetly reversed (he's the one running late, she starts counting) [R10].
- **When asked about something new**, he asks practical questions before he answers ("Like,
  who? Where? Would we— would you want me there?") [R01, M16].
