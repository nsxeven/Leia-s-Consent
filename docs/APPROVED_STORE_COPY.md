## A — main page

A holiday with your gorgeous girlfriend and many new faces. What could possibly go wrong?

Play as Ryan as a getaway with Leia takes many unexpected turns. Other men start noticing her. Small moments become harder to brush off. And the choices you make begin to change the relationship you thought you knew.

Leia’s Consent is an adult visual novel centred on NTR, NTS, cheating and girlfriend sharing—with a slow burn, branching choices and consequences that follow you beyond the moment.

Leia’s Consent is an NTR/NTS visual novel about you, your girlfriend, and a holiday that complicates everything.

Step into Ryan’s shoes as new attention, jealousy and secrets enter your relationship with Leia. Follow the choices that bring you closer, draw others into your relationship, or leave things happening behind your back.
